## Structure and interaction

The explorer has source navigation, the main view and a provenance inspector.
Knowledge graph, Architecture and Inventory are mutually exclusive main views;
Inventory hides the graph SVG and retains pagination and row keyboard interaction.
Search, source filters and the inspector retain full paths/names when drawing
limits or progressive expansion omit labels from the graph.

Graph views support pan, anchored zoom, fit/reset and individual object dragging.
Manual positions are per-view in-memory presentation state, preserved across
filters/live snapshots while open; they do not change source, index or approvals.
Focused nodes can be moved with arrow keys, and Escape cancels dragging.
