# Code of Conduct — Aikya Harness

## Our commitment
Aikya Harness aims to be an open, inclusive, professional and collaborative
community where developers, architects, platform engineers and AI practitioners
can exchange knowledge and improve engineering workflows.

We commit to a respectful environment for everyone, regardless of experience,
background, identity, gender, age, disability, nationality, religion or other
personal attributes.

## Expected behavior
Participants are expected to:
- Treat others with respect, empathy and professionalism.
- Accept constructive feedback and diverse technical opinions.
- Provide clear, evidence-based and solution-oriented feedback.
- Share knowledge and help other contributors.
- Respect copyright, software licenses and attribution obligations.
- Credit the contributions and work of others.
- Prioritize the safety, quality and maintainability of changes.
- Use AI agents and automation responsibly, with appropriate human oversight.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, insults, personal attacks,
deliberately offensive remarks, stalking and unauthorized publication of private
information are unacceptable. Misrepresenting another contributor's work or
using project infrastructure to intimidate others is also prohibited.

## Technical collaboration
Discuss technical decisions with evidence and curiosity. Focus on implementation,
security, testing, performance and maintenance trade-offs rather than individuals.
Do not publish credentials, secrets, user data or exploit details in public issues.

## Scope
This code applies to official Aikya repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
documentation, community discussions and project events.

## Reporting incidents
Report conduct issues privately to project maintainers using the official
contact channel, if available. The historical contact placeholder is
[CONDUCT_EMAIL]; no functioning email address is specified by the repository.

Include relevant context and references without unnecessarily exposing private
information. Reports will be handled discreetly, with disclosure limited to
people who need the information to assess and respond. Absolute confidentiality
cannot be guaranteed in every investigation.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may take proportionate action after reviewing the circumstances,
including private warnings, removal or correction of content, temporary
participation restrictions, or permanent exclusion from official project spaces.

Decisions consider severity, recurrence, context and impact.

## Community commitment
We want Aikya to support safe learning, experimentation, contribution and
innovation. Technical quality and interpersonal respect sustain a productive
open-source community.
